The Inverzia app (Above the Clouds)

The app uses weather data to forecast a morning sea of fog (temperature inversion) for viewpoints in Germany, Austria, Slovakia, the Czech Republic and Poland. It has no user accounts, requires no registration or sign-in, and most data never leaves your device.

Data stored on your device. The app stores locally the selected area (centre and radius), your favourites (name of the place or viewpoint, coordinates and, where available, elevation), the valleys calculated for them, the selected language and, temporarily, downloaded weather forecasts, which are deleted after 24 hours. This data is stored only in your phone’s storage, is not sent to our servers, and we have no access to it.

Device location. The app may ask for access to your location to show you viewpoints and the fog forecast around where you are. The following applies:

  • Access to your location is voluntary. The app asks for it only after you tap “My location” or “Allow location” in the “Show places nearest to you?” prompt under the place search field; before that it explains what the location is for. If you do not grant permission, the app remains fully usable — you choose the area by searching for a place.
  • Location is determined only while you are actively using the app. The app does not track your location in the background and does not record a history of your movements.
  • The precise coordinates are used to find the nearest place directly on the device. The app remembers the location rounded to about 100 metres and, if the phone provides it, the elevation from GPS. If permission has already been granted, the app uses the phone’s last known location (no older than 3 hours) when you tap the search field, to sort the results by distance. This location is kept only in the app’s memory while it runs and is not sent anywhere.
  • Only approximate coordinates, rounded to about 1 km, are sent to download the weather forecast and terrain elevation.
  • You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone’s settings — on iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Inverzia; on Android: Settings → Apps → Inverzia → Permissions → Location.

Compass and barometer. You can turn on a compass that shows the direction of sunrise and of the valley, and an altimeter that measures your elevation with the phone’s barometer while you climb. Both measurements run only while you have them switched on and the screen is open; they take place entirely on the device and are neither sent nor stored. The compass uses the location permission; on iPhone the altimeter uses the “Motion & Fitness” permission and takes the starting elevation from GPS, or you enter it yourself. You can withdraw the “Motion & Fitness” permission on iPhone at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness → Inverzia.

Reading aloud and calendar. On request, the app reads the weather forecast aloud. It uses the voice built into the phone’s system — the text is processed on the device and is not sent anywhere. The moon phase, sunrise and sunset times and the lunar calendar are calculated on the device without an internet connection.

External services. To obtain weather forecasts and terrain elevation, the app calls the interfaces of the Open-Meteo service. It sends the approximate geographic coordinates of the selected place and, when looking for the valley, of points around it (up to 15 km), always rounded to about 1 km. The same applies to the hourly charts and the 4-day forecast. The inversion map downloads data for a fixed grid of points across whole countries — your location is not used for it at all. The app sends no device identifier and no information that would identify you. Open-Meteo’s servers are located in the European Union. Apart from that, the app itself only downloads current images of some webcams (see below); the lists of municipalities, peaks, tourist places and viewpoints are part of the installation package. Some viewpoints have webcams — for cameras of ČHMÚ and foto-webcam.eu, the app downloads the current image directly from the operator’s server when you tap one (without coordinates and without any information about you; as with any download, the server only sees the IP address); for the others, the app’s window shows the website of the operator (e.g. Panomax, Feratel, Pfänderbahn, SHMÚ), and the “Route” buttons open route planning on Mapy.com with the viewpoint as the destination. Your device loads these websites like a normal browser, and their operators’ terms apply (e.g. cookie consent); the app itself sends them no data.

What the app does not do. It shows no ads, collects no diagnostic or analytics data, builds no advertising profiles, does not sell your data or share it with third parties, does not read your contacts, messages or photos, and does not track you outside the app. The app is free and contains no purchases.

How to delete your data. Remove favourites in the app by tapping the star. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all locally stored data from your device.

The GymDict app

GymDict is a voice workout log for the gym. It has no user accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Speech is recognized on the device without an internet connection, microphone audio is never stored or sent, and workouts are stored only on the device. The app uses the internet only to download the speech model once from dorotty.cloud, which redirects to files hosted on GitHub; as with any download, these providers only see technical connection data. Full policy: GymDict Privacy Policy.

Legal bases

We process personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR — providing the feature you requested, including calculating the forecast for the selected place.
  • Legal obligation under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR — in particular obligations under Slovak accounting and tax law.
  • Legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR — operating and securing the service and handling requests.
  • Consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR where we ask for it — for example by granting the system permission to access your location. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

How long we keep data

We do not store your location on our side. Data in the app, including the coordinates of favourites, stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. We keep accounting records for ten years as required by law. We keep email correspondence until the request is handled and then for at most two years.
