Zásady ochrany súkromia – Turista

Účinné od 27. októbra 2026

Appku Turista – sprievodca po horách (ďalej „appka“) vydáva spoločnosť Dorotty, s. r. o. Tu sa dozviete, aké údaje appka spracúva, kam a prečo odchádzajú, ako dlho sa uchovávajú a aké máte práva.

Stručne

  • Appka nemá účty ani prihlásenie, reklamy, analytiku ani sledovanie a neobsahuje nákupy. Nemá ani vlastný server na údaje používateľov.
  • Hľadanie cieľa aj výpočet trás prebiehajú priamo v mobile. Uložené túry, nastavenia a mapy trás ostávajú len v mobile, nemáme k nim prístup.
  • Polohu appka používa len na obrazovke vlastnej navigácie „Turista offline“, na obrazovke Bezpečnosť, pre nadmorskú výšku na úvodnej obrazovke a v nástrojoch (kompas, výškomer, Aký to je kopec), nikdy nie na pozadí, a nikam ju neposiela.
  • Fotoaparát (len v nástroji Aký to je kopec) a snímače mobilu (natočenie, kompas, barometer) appka spracúva len v mobile, nič nenahráva a nikam neposiela.
  • Na počasie posiela službe Open-Meteo súradnice štartu a najvyššieho bodu trasy zaokrúhlené na približne 1 km – nie vašu polohu.
  • Mapu sťahuje zo služby OpenFreeMap a z nášho mapového servera, výstrahy SHMÚ z verejného kanála MeteoAlarm. Tieto služby dostanú len samotnú požiadavku a IP adresu zariadenia.

2. Údaje, ktoré ostávajú v mobile

  • Poloha zariadenia (GPS): vlastná navigácia „Turista offline“ (poloha na mape, pokyny na trase, upozornenie pri zídení z trasy), vaše súradnice na obrazovke Bezpečnosť na nadiktovanie dispečerovi a nástroje: kompas (zemepisný sever a smer k vybranému cieľu), výškomer (nadmorská výška podľa GPS a značeného chodníka – aj v rámiku na úvodnej obrazovke, kde sa poloha zisťuje raz za 5 minút, kým je obrazovka otvorená) a Aký to je kopec (výhľad z miesta, kde stojíte). Poloha je len v pamäti mobilu, kým je niektorá z týchto obrazoviek otvorená; neukladá sa a nikam sa neposiela.
  • Obraz z fotoaparátu: v nástroji Aký to je kopec appka zobrazuje živý obraz zadnej kamery a k nemu mená vrcholov vypočítané v mobile. Obraz sa nenahráva ani neposiela. Aby mená sedeli aj pri nepresnom kompase, appka, keď mobil držíte v pokoji, urobí tichú zmenšenú snímku, v mobile ju porovná s vypočítaným obzorom a hneď ju zmaže. Keď obraz zastavíte tlačidlom, fotka je v dočasnej pamäti mobilu, kým sa nevrátite k živému obrazu alebo nezatvoríte obrazovku; ak ju chcete poslať, otvorí sa systémové zdieľanie (bod 5).
  • Snímače mobilu (natočenie a pohyb, magnetometer, barometer): smer a sklon mobilu pre kompas a Aký to je kopec, tlak vzduchu pre výškomer. Spracúvajú sa len v pamäti mobilu, kým je obrazovka otvorená; nikam sa neposielajú. Výškomer si pamätá len dorovnanie barometra a priebeh výšky do zatvorenia appky.
  • Zorné pole kamery: appka si ho v mobile zapamätá, keď ho zistí pri zarovnávaní podľa obzoru alebo keď ho zmeníte štipnutím, aby mená sedeli aj nabudúce. Posun mien prstom platí len do zatvorenia obrazovky.
  • Hľadaný cieľ a vypočítané trasy: výstupy, okruhy a prechody sa počítajú v mobile zo siete chodníkov, ktorá je súčasťou appky. To, čo hľadáte, sa nikam neposiela.
  • Moje túry (cieľ, štart, dátum a čas odchodu): zoznam uložených túr. Sú v mobile, kým túru nezmažete alebo appku neodinštalujete.
  • Pripomienka túry: ak ste upozornenia povolili, deň pred túrou o 18:00 príde upozornenie s cieľom, štartom a časom odchodu. Naplánuje ho systém mobilu, nič sa pritom neposiela cez internet; so zmazaním túry sa zruší.
  • Mapa trasy stiahnutá do mobilu: mapa a navigácia bez signálu. Je v mobile do odinštalovania appky.
  • Stiahnuté predpovede počasia a výstrahy: aby ste ich mali aj bez signálu. Po dni túry ich appka pri ďalšom spustení zmaže.
  • Nastavenia (jazyk, tempo chôdze, predvolený čas odchodu, spôsob navigácie, zorné pole kamery): fungovanie appky. Sú v mobile do odinštalovania appky.

Tieto údaje sa neodosielajú na naše servery a nemáme k nim prístup. Odinštalovaním appky sa z mobilu natrvalo zmažú. Ak máte zapnutú zálohu mobilu (iCloud, Google), môže záloha obsahovať aj údaje appky podľa nastavení vášho mobilu.

3. Čo appka posiela na internet

  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) – na predpoveď počasia na deň túry dostane súradnice štartu a najvyššieho bodu vybranej trasy zaokrúhlené na približne 1 km, ich nadmorskú výšku a dátum túry. Nie vašu polohu.
  • MeteoAlarm (feeds.meteoalarm.org) – na výstrahy SHMÚ dostane len požiadavku na verejný zoznam výstrah pre Slovensko a na úplný text výstrah pre okresy na trase. Ktoré výstrahy sa týkajú trasy, appka vyberá v mobile.
  • OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org) a náš mapový server na Cloudflare, ktorý zdieľame s našou appkou Divočina (divocina.dorotty.cloud, mapa.divocina.dorotty.cloud) – na zobrazenie mapy a stiahnutie mapy trasy do mobilu dostanú požiadavku na časť mapy (súradnice mapových dlaždíc), ktorú si prezeráte alebo sťahujete.

Ako pri každej požiadavke na internete vidí každá z týchto služieb aj IP adresu vášho zariadenia a technické údaje o pripojení (čas, typ požiadavky). Appka im neposiela identifikátor zariadenia, meno ani iný údaj, ktorý by vás identifikoval. Pri vlastnej navigácii ukazuje mapa okolie miesta, kde práve ste, takže z požiadavky na túto časť mapy sa dá odvodiť oblasť, v ktorej sa nachádzate (nie vaše presné súradnice). Komunikácia je šifrovaná (HTTPS). Kto tieto služby prevádzkuje, je v bode 8.

6. Povolenia

  • Poloha (len pri používaní appky): appka o ňu požiada na úvodnej obrazovke (nadmorská výška), na obrazovke vlastnej navigácie, Bezpečnosť alebo v nástrojoch. Polohu na pozadí nepoužíva. Bez povolenia ostatné funkcie fungujú, len vlastná navigácia, súradnice v Bezpečnosti a výhľad z miesta, kde stojíte, nie (kompas ukáže magnetický sever). Povolenie môžete kedykoľvek odobrať – na iPhone: Nastavenia → Súkromie a bezpečnosť → Polohové služby → Turista; v systéme Android: Nastavenia → Aplikácie → Turista → Povolenia → Poloha.
  • Fotoaparát: len v nástroji Aký to je kopec, appka oň požiada až tam. Bez povolenia ukáže výhľad ako 3D obraz krajiny. Mikrofón appka nepoužíva. Povolenie odoberiete v nastaveniach mobilu (Turista → Fotoaparát).
  • Pohyb a kondícia (iPhone): výškomer číta barometer cez systémové rozhranie pohybu. Krokomer ani údaje o aktivite appka nepoužíva. V systéme Android snímače povolenie nepotrebujú.
  • Upozornenia: pripomienka uloženej túry. Appka o ne požiada pri uložení túry; bez nich sa túra uloží, len bez pripomienky. Vypnete ich v nastaveniach mobilu.
  • Vibrácia: upozornenie pri vlastnej navigácii, keď zídete z trasy.
  • Internet: mapa, počasie a výstrahy (bod 3).

Privacy Policy – Turista

Effective from 4 November 2026

The Slovak version is authoritative; this translation is provided for your information.

Turista – mountain hiking guide (the “app”) is published by Dorotty, s. r. o. Here you will find out what data the app processes, where they go and why, how long they are kept and what rights you have.

In short

  • The app has no accounts or sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no purchases. It also has no server of its own for user data.
  • Searching for a destination and calculating routes take place directly on your phone. Saved hikes, settings and route maps stay only on your phone; we have no access to them.
  • The app uses your location only on the screen of its own navigation, “Turista offline”, on the Safety screen, for the altitude on the home screen and in the tools (compass, altimeter, What’s that peak?), never in the background, and does not send it anywhere.
  • The app processes images from the camera (only in the What’s that peak? tool) and data from the phone’s sensors (orientation, compass, barometer) only on the phone; it records nothing and sends nothing anywhere.
  • For the weather, it sends the Open-Meteo service the coordinates of the start and of the highest point of the route, rounded to about 1 km – not your location.
  • It downloads the map from the OpenFreeMap service and from our map server, and the weather warnings of SHMÚ (the Slovak Hydrometeorological Institute) from the public MeteoAlarm feed. These services receive only the request itself and the device’s IP address.

1. Who processes the data

The controller is Dorotty, s. r. o., with its registered office at M. R. Štefánika 1066/6, 018 41 Dubnica nad Váhom, Slovakia, company ID (IČO) 46 651 951, registered in the Commercial Register of the District Court Trenčín, section Sro, file no. 26160/R (“we”).

Contact for questions about personal data: info@dorotty.cloud. We have not appointed a data protection officer, as Article 37 GDPR does not require us to do so.

2. Data that stay on your phone

  • Device location (GPS): the app’s own navigation, “Turista offline” (your position on the map, directions along the route, a warning when you leave the route), your coordinates on the Safety screen to read out to the dispatcher, and the tools: compass (true north and the direction to a chosen destination), altimeter (altitude based on GPS and the marked trail – also in the box on the home screen, where the location is checked once every 5 minutes while that screen is open) and What’s that peak? (the view from where you are standing). The location is held only in the phone’s memory while one of these screens is open; it is not stored and is not sent anywhere.
  • Camera image: in the What’s that peak? tool, the app shows the live image from the rear camera together with the names of peaks calculated on the phone. The image is not recorded or sent. So that the names fit even when the compass is inaccurate, the app takes a silent, reduced-size snapshot when you hold the phone still, compares it on the phone with the calculated horizon and deletes it immediately. When you freeze the image with the button, the photo stays in the phone’s temporary storage until you return to the live image or close the screen; if you want to send it, the system share sheet opens (section 5).
  • Phone sensors (orientation and motion, magnetometer, barometer): the direction and tilt of the phone for the compass and What’s that peak?, and air pressure for the altimeter. They are processed only in the phone’s memory while the screen is open; they are not sent anywhere. The altimeter remembers only the barometer calibration and the altitude history until the app is closed.
  • Camera field of view: the app remembers it on the phone when it determines it while aligning to the horizon or when you change it by pinching, so that the names fit next time too. Moving the names with your finger applies only until the screen is closed.
  • Searched destination and calculated routes: ascents, loops and traverses are calculated on the phone from the trail network that is part of the app. What you search for is not sent anywhere.
  • My hikes (destination, start, date and departure time): the list of saved hikes. They stay on the phone until you delete the hike or uninstall the app.
  • Hike reminder: if you have allowed notifications, a notification with the destination, start and departure time arrives at 18:00 the day before the hike. It is scheduled by the phone’s system and nothing is sent over the internet; it is cancelled when you delete the hike.
  • Route map downloaded to the phone: map and navigation without signal. It stays on the phone until the app is uninstalled.
  • Downloaded weather forecasts and warnings: so that you have them even without signal. After the day of the hike, the app deletes them the next time it is started.
  • Settings (language, walking pace, default departure time, navigation method, camera field of view): for the app to work. They stay on the phone until the app is uninstalled.

These data are not sent to our servers and we have no access to them. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes them from the phone. If you have phone backup turned on (iCloud, Google), the backup may also contain the app’s data, depending on your phone’s settings.

3. What the app sends over the internet

  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) – for the weather forecast for the day of the hike, it receives the coordinates of the start and of the highest point of the selected route, rounded to about 1 km, their elevation and the date of the hike. Not your location.
  • MeteoAlarm (feeds.meteoalarm.org) – for SHMÚ warnings, it receives only a request for the public list of warnings for Slovakia and for the full text of the warnings for the districts along the route. The app selects on the phone which warnings concern the route.
  • OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org) and our map server on Cloudflare, which we share with our Divočina app (divocina.dorotty.cloud, mapa.divocina.dorotty.cloud) – to display the map and to download the route map to the phone, they receive a request for the part of the map (the coordinates of map tiles) that you are viewing or downloading.

As with any request on the internet, each of these services also sees your device’s IP address and technical connection data (time, type of request). The app does not send them a device identifier, your name or any other data that would identify you. During the app’s own navigation, the map shows the surroundings of where you are, so the area you are in (not your exact coordinates) can be inferred from the request for that part of the map. Communication is encrypted (HTTPS). Who operates these services is set out in section 8.

4. Links to other apps and websites

These links open only when you tap them. After that, the data are processed by the app or website concerned under its own policy, not by us.

  • Mapy.com (a third-party app or website) – navigation along the route, “Drive to the start” and accommodation on the map. The link contains the coordinates of the start, the waypoints and the destination of the route, or of the place shown – not your location. For “Drive to the start” it contains only the coordinates of the start of the route; Mapy.com itself determines where to plan the journey from, based on your settings in its app.
  • Google Maps – “By public transport”: the link contains the coordinates of the start of the route.
  • Accommodation – the website, phone number or email of the establishment according to OpenStreetMap. We receive no commission for these links.
  • App Store and Google Play – the page of the official Mountain Rescue Service (HZS) app and of our Inverzia app. If you have Inverzia installed, the app opens it directly at the viewpoint.
  • Websites laviny.sk, hzs.sk and tanap.sk and the websites of the data sources listed in the app under Sources and licences.

The 18 300 and 112 buttons only open the dialler on your phone. The call goes through your mobile operator, not through us.

5. Sharing and reporting an error

  • Send location (Safety), Send GPX (route detail) and sharing a photo with peak names or a picture of the view (What’s that peak?) open the system share sheet. You decide to whom and how you send the message or file; we receive nothing. The location message contains your coordinates, the GPS accuracy and a link to the place on Mapy.com; the GPX file contains the route with its start and destination; and the picture of the view contains a photo or a 3D image of the landscape with peak names (without coordinates).
  • Report an error in a route or in the app (Settings) opens your email app with a message to info@dorotty.cloud; the subject contains the app version. You send the email yourself. We process your address and the content of the message only to handle your request.

6. Permissions

  • Location (only while using the app): the app asks for it on the home screen (altitude), on the screen of its own navigation, on the Safety screen or in the tools. It does not use location in the background. Without the permission the other features work; only the app’s own navigation, the coordinates on the Safety screen and the view from where you are standing do not (the compass shows magnetic north). You can withdraw the permission at any time – on iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Turista; on Android: Settings → Apps → Turista → Permissions → Location.
  • Camera: only in the What’s that peak? tool; the app asks for it only there. Without the permission it shows the view as a 3D image of the landscape. The app does not use the microphone. You can withdraw the permission in your phone’s settings (Turista → Camera).
  • Motion & Fitness (iPhone): the altimeter reads the barometer through the system motion interface. The app does not use the step counter or activity data. On Android, the sensors need no permission.
  • Notifications: reminder of a saved hike. The app asks for them when you save a hike; without them the hike is saved, just without a reminder. You can turn them off in your phone’s settings.
  • Vibration: an alert during the app’s own navigation when you leave the route.
